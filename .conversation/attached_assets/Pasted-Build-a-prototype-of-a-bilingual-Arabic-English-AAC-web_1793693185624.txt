Build a prototype of a bilingual (Arabic/English) AAC web app for children with autism. Use React + Tailwind + React Router. No backend: store everything locally in IndexedDB (photos and audio as blobs) so it works offline as a PWA.

DESIGN
- Calm pastel colors, large touch targets (72px+), no ads or flashing, readable contrast (WCAG AA), respect reduced-motion.
- AR/EN switcher on every page. Arabic = RTL, English = LTR, all text from translation files, language remembered.

PAGE 1 - Welcome: friendly message, big "Start" button, language toggle.

PAGE 2 - Board:
- Top "I Want" bar: shows selected cards, with buttons: Speak sentence, Delete last, Clear all.
- Tabs: Core Words (yes, no, help, finished, more, stop, need a break, feelings), Fruits & Vegetables, Personal Items, My Body.
- Cards: image on top, bold label below, same layout for default and custom cards (emoji placeholders for defaults).
- Tap card: soft highlight, instantly play the parent's voice recording for the current language (fallback to browser speech), and add it to the bar.
- Always-visible Help/Pain button.
- My Body tab: child's photo (or neutral avatar) with tappable hotspots. Tapping one plays its recorded name, adds "It hurts: [part]" to the bar, then shows a simple face-based pain scale.

PAGE 3 - Caregiver panel:
- Opened from a small lock icon with a 2-second long-press, then a 4-digit PIN (created on first use, stored hashed, locked 60s after 5 wrong tries).
- Add/edit/delete cards: take or upload a photo (compressed), Arabic and English labels, and a voice recording per language (MediaRecorder with cross-browser format support, record/stop/play/re-record).
- Body map setup: choose the child's photo (with a privacy tip) or neutral avatar, tap to place hotspots, drag/resize them, name them in both languages, and record their voices.
- Settings: volume, language, delete all data.

Build it in stages: (1) setup, i18n, routing, storage; (2) welcome page and board; (3) caregiver panel; (4) body map; (5) PWA. Confirm each stage works before moving on.
